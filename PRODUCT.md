# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, collaborators, and curious visitors landing on the personal
company site of Jacob Nilsson (Handsala AB), a developer in Gothenburg, Sweden.
They arrive to answer "who is this person, what have they built, how do I reach
them" — but the site should reward lingering, not just scanning. Visitors who
poke around should keep finding things.

## Product Purpose

Handsala AB's public face: portfolio ("the sagas"), blog, and contact. The name
is Old Icelandic — *handsala (að), v. to make over (confirm) by shaking hands* —
and the whole identity hangs off that: deals sealed by hand, craft, sagas,
manuscripts. Success = a visitor remembers the site, explores it for fun, and
reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, mischievous. A developer who thinks debugging in a café and
renovating a house in the woods belong in the same sentence. Self-deprecating
humor is canon ("I don't sleep, I generate puzzles"). Maximalism through scale,
color commitment, and interaction, never through themed props. The Old
Icelandic etymology is a fact stated once, not an aesthetic to roleplay.
The site should feel *fun to explore* — hidden interactions and easter eggs are
a feature requirement, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero metric, three-card feature grid, gradient text.
- Beige minimalism with timid slate text (the previous design).
- Corporate portfolio templates; anything a recruiter has seen 400 times.
- Viking/medieval cosplay (confirmed by owner 2026-07-28): no wax seals,
  fleurons, folio marks, "saga/scribe" copy, manuscript ornament, or blackletter
  energy. Runes are allowed only sparingly, as hidden easter eggs (max ~2 on a
  page), never as visible decoration or bullets.
- Fantasy-kitsch generally: no parchment textures, no Papyrus energy.

## Design Principles

1. **The manuscript is the interface.** Rubrication (red for emphasis), folio
   marks, drop caps, margin notes, a colophon. Structure borrowed from real
   codices, executed with modern precision.
2. **Reward exploration.** Every section hides at least one interaction or
   joke that is only found by hovering, clicking, or reading the margins.
3. **Commit to the drench.** Sections own their color (ink, vellum, oxblood).
   No hedging with neutrals.
4. **The scribe has a voice.** Copy is first-person, funny, and specific.
   Margin notes talk back to the main text.
5. **Craft over chrome.** Ornament is drawn (SVG, CSS), never pasted textures
   or stock flourishes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on all body text, including drenched sections.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables marquees and entrance choreography.
- All easter eggs are bonus content — nothing essential is hidden behind them.
- Semantic landmarks and keyboard-reachable interactive elements throughout.
