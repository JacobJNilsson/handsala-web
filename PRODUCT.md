# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, collaborators, and curious visitors landing on the personal
company site of Jacob Nilsson (Handsala AB), a developer in Gothenburg, Sweden.
They arrive to answer "who is this person, what have they built, how do I reach
them". The site should reward lingering, not just scanning. Visitors who poke
around should keep finding things.

## Product Purpose

Handsala AB's public face: portfolio, blog, and contact. The name is Old
Icelandic, *handsala (að), v. to make over (confirm) by shaking hands*. That
etymology is a fact the site states once, in the hero definition. It is not an
aesthetic to roleplay. Success = a visitor remembers the site, explores it for
fun, and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, mischievous. A developer who thinks debugging in a café and
renovating a house in the woods belong in the same sentence. Maximalism through
scale, color commitment, typography, and interaction, never through themed
props. The site should feel *fun to explore*: hidden interactions and easter
eggs are a feature requirement, not decoration.

## Voice and copy ownership

The site's humor is Jacob's own ("I don't sleep, I generate puzzles"). Agents
and tools must not write jokes, quips, or wordplay into site copy. Propose copy
as suggestions in conversation; only Jacob's approved words ship. Functional
copy (labels, alt text, error messages) is fine to write, in plain language.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: hero metric, three-card feature grid, gradient text.
- Beige minimalism with timid slate text (the previous design).
- Corporate portfolio templates; anything a recruiter has seen 400 times.
- Viking/medieval cosplay (confirmed by owner 2026-07-28): no wax seals,
  fleurons, folio marks, drop caps, margin notes, "saga/scribe" copy,
  manuscript ornament, or blackletter energy. Runes are allowed only sparingly,
  as hidden easter eggs (max ~2 on a page), never as visible decoration or
  bullets.
- Typographic reference marks as decoration: no daggers (†), asterisk chains,
  or footnote apparatus (confirmed by owner 2026-08-03).
- Fantasy-kitsch generally: no parchment textures, no Papyrus energy.
- Generated humor in site copy (confirmed by owner 2026-08-03). It reads as
  cringe. See "Voice and copy ownership".

## Design Principles

1. **Editorial register.** Light sharp serif display (Sentient) with a quiet
   grotesk (Archivo). Scale carries the drama: huge display type, strong
   weight contrast, confident whitespace. Reference: the Jennifer Heintz
   editorial school, not manuscript facsimile.
2. **Reward exploration.** Sections hide interactions that are only found by
   hovering or clicking. Never label or explain them; discovery must be
   unprompted. Post-discovery reward messages are fine.
3. **Commit to the drench.** Sections own their color (ink, vellum,
   vermillion). No hedging with neutrals.
4. **Craft over chrome.** Ornament is drawn (SVG, CSS), never pasted textures
   or stock flourishes. The handshake artwork is the house mark; it earns its
   screen time through motion and precision, not repetition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on all body text, including drenched sections.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the intro animation and entrance
  choreography.
- All easter eggs are bonus content; nothing essential is hidden behind them.
- Semantic landmarks and keyboard-reachable interactive elements throughout.
