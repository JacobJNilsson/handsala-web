Privacy Policy

For the Palette puzzle game · Last updated: 8 July 2026

Palette is a color puzzle game made by Handsala. We built it to be simple and privacy‑respecting: we do not require an account and we do not collect personal information about you. This policy explains the limited data that the app and the third‑party services it uses may process.

The short version

  • We don't ask you to sign in, and we don't collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device and are not sent to us.
  • The app shows ads via Google AdMob, which uses a device/advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. Ads are configured as non‑personalized and family‑safe.
  • If you choose to support the developer, payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • We use no other analytics or tracking.

Who we are

This app and this policy are provided by Handsala ("we", "us"). The app is published on Google Play under the package name se.handsala.spectrum. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@handsala.com.

Information we do not collect

Palette has no user accounts and no login. We do not ask for, and do not collect, personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or health data.

Data stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves a small amount of information locally on your device using standard app storage. This includes:

  • Your current puzzle progress and puzzle history
  • Preferences such as theme and how cell values are displayed
  • Your win statistics
  • Whether you have seen the in‑app tutorial

This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Palette displays ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob software may collect and process certain information from your device, including:

  • A device or advertising identifier (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Diagnostic and technical information used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention

We configure advertising in Palette to be non‑personalized and family‑safe, which limits the use of identifiers for ad personalization. This processing is carried out by Google as a third party. To learn how Google collects and uses data from apps that use its services, see “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services” and the Google Privacy Policy.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

Payments (Stripe)

Supporting the developer is entirely optional. If you choose to make a payment, it is processed by Stripe. Card and payment details are entered into and handled directly by Stripe's secure payment interface — we do not receive, see, or store your card number. Stripe's handling of that information is governed by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics, crash‑reporting, or user‑tracking services of our own beyond the ad and payment providers described above.

How your data is shared

We do not sell your data. The only third parties that process data through the app are the service providers named above:

Children's privacy

Palette is a general‑audience puzzle game and is suitable for a broad audience. Because we serve only non‑personalized, family‑safe ads and do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly build profiles of children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@handsala.com and we will address it.

Your rights and choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or limit ad personalization, in your device settings.
  • You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since we hold no account data ourselves, most such requests relate to the third‑party providers above; you can also contact us and we will help direct your request.

Data retention

We do not retain personal data on our own servers. Locally stored game data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google and Stripe is retained according to their respective policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
